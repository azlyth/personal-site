{# The two values a link preview needs, derived once and shared. A post is usually READ as a card first -- on Reddit, in iMessage, in a feed reader -- and the card is built from `og:description` and `og:image`. base.html renders those into the page head and rss.xml renders the same two values into the feed, so they live here rather than in both: the failure mode of duplicating them is that the feed and the card slowly disagree about what a post is, which nobody notices because neither is visible from the site itself.
